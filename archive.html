01 / Practice

Action, movement, thought.

The current artist website describes the work as centered on action, speed of movement and thought. Dynamic performances are documented through various techniques and become source material for completed two-dimensional narratives depicting motion. citeturn0search0

Working description. The archive connects the early video/performance work of the late 1990s and early 2000s with the later digital-print practice. The historical record includes performance, video, photography, painting, mixed media, film, poetry and design, while the current body of work emphasizes movement and narrative in two-dimensional images.

INTERSTATE: appears in both 2022 and 2023 records and may represent a continued exhibition period or CV duplication; original gallery records should establish the exact date.
